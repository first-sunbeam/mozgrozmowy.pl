## A Bug in the Algorithm

For years, my system asked three questions:

1. **Can I do it?**  
   Almost always: yes.
2. **Is there a solution?**  
   Almost always: yes.
3. **Can I keep going?**  
   Usually: yes.

If the answer was “yes”, the problem was considered solved.

Except my system was leaving out one question:

**Is the outcome worth the cost of continuing?**

And that changes a surprising amount.

Being able to endure something is not a reason to endure it.

The existence of a solution does not mean the solution is worth its cost.

And “I can do it” tells me only that something is feasible. It tells me nothing about whether it’s worth it.
