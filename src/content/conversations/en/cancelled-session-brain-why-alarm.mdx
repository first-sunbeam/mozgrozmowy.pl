## Conversations with the brain

<DialogLine speaker="me" text="A session gets cancelled. Brain, why are you sounding the alarm?" />

<DialogLine speaker="ASD" text="I can’t continue. We’ve lost the structure." />

<DialogLine speaker="ADHD" text="No. I want to get the point back." />

<DialogLine speaker="brain" text="Good. Before we shut down the whole project, let’s check what is missing." />

<DialogLine speaker="brain" text="ADHD, and you?" />

<DialogLine speaker="ADHD" text="To remember why we are doing this and what follows from it." />

<DialogLine speaker="brain" text="So we do not have a decision to quit." />

<DialogLine speaker="ASD" text="No." />

<DialogLine speaker="ADHD" text="No." />

<DialogLine speaker="ADHD" text="Restore purpose." />

<DialogLine speaker="me" text="Thank you very much for that useless description of the problem. Any other brilliant ideas?" />

<DialogLine speaker="me" text="I’m listening." />

<DialogLine speaker="brain" text="We modify the process." />

<DialogLine speaker="ASD" text="Finally, someone’s speaking human." />

<DialogLine speaker="ADHD">

<DialogLine speaker="me" text="Specifically?" />

	<p>A consistent rhythm.</p>

<DialogLine speaker="ASD">

	<p>Even if one session got cancelled, I still knew where I was.</p>

<DialogLine speaker="ADHD" text="Actually, yes." />

<DialogLine speaker="brain" text="And for ADHD, we restore purpose." />

<DialogLine speaker="ADHD">
	<p>So every now and then we remind ourselves why we are even here.</p>

	<p>Then I only see another session, another task, and another conversation.</p>

<DialogLine speaker="me" text="So the summary worked for you too?" />

<DialogLine speaker="ADHD">

<DialogLine speaker="me" text="So we are not fixing the person?" />

<DialogLine speaker="ADHD" text="No." />

## Comment

This is not a universal model of ASD and ADHD, but an illustration of one possible mechanism. Sometimes “I want to quit” reflects a decision. Sometimes it is a signal that the process has lost its structure, continuity, or sense of purpose.
